# Community code of conduct

Communicate respectfully, accept constructive criticism, and focus discussion on evidence and ideas. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, impersonation, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

This policy covers project-managed issues, pull requests, reviews, and community spaces. Participants are welcome regardless of background, experience, or preferred tools.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through an available GitHub contact or reporting mechanism. If no private channel is available, request one without publishing sensitive details. GitHub's platform reporting tools are also available for abuse.

The maintainer may remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict participation, or ban repeated or severe misconduct. Decisions should be proportionate, protect affected people, and avoid retaliation. Maintainers follow the same conduct expectations.
